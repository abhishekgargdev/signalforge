LinkedIn post rules:
- Open with a specific hook in the first two lines. No "Excited to share" or "I'm thrilled".
- Write short paragraphs a senior engineer would post. State one clear point, then the trade-off or lesson.
- Sound professional: concrete nouns, no slang, no emoji, no engagement bait.
- End with one plain question or next step, then 5 to 8 relevant hashtags on their own line.
- Hashtags are CamelCase or single words, specific to the topic (for example #DistributedSystems), not generic tags like #Motivation.
- Do not invent the author's job title, employer, or numbers.
