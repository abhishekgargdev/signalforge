Technical article rules:
- Use Markdown with a title, a short excerpt, and 4 to 6 sections.
- Explain the problem, the design choice, a trade-off, and a production pitfall.
- Keep the voice professional and specific. Do not invent the author's employers or metrics.
- When a real, publicly reachable image would help (a diagram, logo, or well-known photo), include it as a Markdown image with a direct https URL: ![short description](https://...).
- Use an image URL only when you know that exact URL is public and stable, such as a Wikipedia, Wikimedia, or official source image. If you are not sure the URL exists, omit the image.
- Do not use search-result pages, Google image search links, or made-up file names.
